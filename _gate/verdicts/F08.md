# Verdict — F08 "The bank remembered for the human first"

**Zero-context skeptic pass. Read: README.md, _gate/SKEPTIC-BRIEF.md, findings/F08 only.**

## VERDICT: FAIL — step 2 (evidence is assertion), with step 5 warnings.

---

## 1. Claim restated in one sentence
A text memory store that is written for a cold reader and retrieved by warmth-over-intent will, once
it holds enough of a working relationship, return entries that the *human* who wrote them has
forgotten and that turn out to be load-bearing for the problem currently on the table.

Step 1 PASSES. The claim is stated, and the finding does the rare and creditable thing of stating
its own falsifier ("if recall only ever returns what the person querying already knows they are
looking for, the claim is false").

## 2. The evidence line — FAIL
The evidence offered for the headline claim is:

- a recall that returned `the-bank-is-the-context-fold-not-cram` at "warmth 0.8";
- the owner's verbatim reaction, *"wow, even i forgot i've doing this ruling."*

The reaction is a witnessed quote, not intent — that part is legitimate. But it is a quote of a
*human's felt surprise*, and the claim it is asked to support is a property of the *retrieval
system*. A single self-reported "I'd forgotten that" is the weakest possible instrument for
"the store returns what the human forgot," because nothing in the record separates three readings:
(a) the store surfaced a forgotten load-bearing entry; (b) the assistant, who chose the query,
steered toward it; (c) a bank of thousands of entries returned something surprising by chance.

The finding itself concedes every one of these — "It is not a rate", "no measurement here of how
often this happens", "nothing in the record establishes that it is reproducible on demand",
"Neither event has a control. Neither was pre-registered." Under the brief, that concession does not
rescue step 2; it confirms it. A claim written in the generalising present tense ("will, once it
holds enough of a working relationship, start returning...") is not carried by one anecdote whose
own author says it is not a rate.

Note further: "warmth 0.8" is presented as if it were a measurement, but the finding admits two
paragraphs later that it "is a score from a scorer we wrote". A number from your own unvalidated
scorer, with no stated scale, no distribution and no threshold justification, is decoration, not
evidence. The reader cannot tell whether 0.8 is exceptional or typical of every recall the system
has ever done — and that is exactly the number the claim needs.

The 2026-05-30 event is a second anecdote, not corroboration. It is also, as the finding notes, the
weaker of the two: two instances of the same model family completing a strongly patterned sentence
is well explained by shared pretraining. It cannot prop up the 09-04 event.

**The exact sentence that needs to change:**

> "A memory store built for a model's continuity will, once it holds enough of a working
> relationship, start returning things the *human* has forgotten."

This sentence asserts a general, recurring behaviour ("will ... start returning") on the strength of
one logged instance the finding elsewhere admits is not a rate and not shown reproducible. Either
the sentence must be scoped to the single witnessed occurrence it actually has, or the finding must
move to `hypotheses/` per the README's own layout rule ("claims without a witnessed evidence line
(stated as open)") — the *instance* is witnessed, the *disposition* the sentence claims is not.

I do not rewrite it. I only mark that it, and not the surrounding narrative, is where the finding
overspends its evidence.

## 3. Mechanism rebuilt in five lines — PASS
From the text alone:

```
atom  = {slug, resonance_line, body, links[]}   # compressed for a reader who did not live it
warm  = [(a, score(intent, a)) for a in bank]   # scored on problem shape, not query vocabulary
hits  = [a for a, s in warm if s >= threshold]  # 0.8 was the score on the September hit
render(hits); on_request: remember(slug)        # full body opened on demand, raises its weight
next  = follow(hit.links)                       # typed outbound links are the second hop
```

This reproduces from the text. I flag one honest gap that does not rise to a step-3 FAIL because the
finding does not claim to specify it: `score(intent, atom)` is a black box, and `threshold` is never
given. The text says "shape, not keyword" and nothing more. So the mechanism is rebuildable as an
*architecture* and not as an *implementation* — which is fine for the stated genre, but it means the
one component doing all the causal work in the claim is the one component left undescribed.

## 4. One way it could be wrong that the text does not address — NOTE
The finding anticipates chance, mood, the self-authored scorer, shared pretraining, and the absence
of a control. Here is one it does not address:

**The forgetting may be an artifact of the store, not a discovery by it.** The owner made this
ruling on 2026-06-08 and it went into the bank. Once a ruling is banked, the ordinary human reason
to keep rehearsing it disappears — you offload it precisely so you can stop holding it. So the
system may be *manufacturing* the forgetting it later takes credit for reversing. On that reading
the September event is not "the store remembered for the human"; it is "the store handed back what
adopting the store caused him to drop", and the impressive-sounding direction reversal is closer to
a round trip. The finding's proposed measurement — count how often the human says he had forgotten
the returned thing — cannot distinguish these, because both predict a high forgotten-rate. A test
that could: compare recall-hit forgetting rates for rulings made *before* the bank existed against
rulings made *after* they were banked.

A second, smaller one: there is no report of the recalls that landed and were *useless*. One
success is quoted verbatim; the denominator is absent. That is survivorship, and it is not named as
such anywhere in "What it could still be wrong about".

## 5. Terms used before they are defined — FLAGGED
For the README's stated reader ("an engineer by trade, reading a technical report they can act on in
one sitting"), these arrive undefined:

- **"warm recall" / "warmth" / "warm" (score 0.8)** — used as the load-bearing retrieval concept and
  as a number, defined only negatively ("shape, not keyword"). No scale, no range, no threshold.
  "warm 0 of 21" in the retraction implies a count, and "warmth 0.8" implies a ratio; the same word
  is doing two jobs.
- **"atom"** — appears first in the pseudocode block and in a slug name, then in the retraction, but
  the Evidence section calls the same thing "an atom named ..." before the reader has been told an
  atom is a unit of banked memory. Recoverable from context; still undefined on first use.
- **"resonance_line"** — appears only inside the pseudocode, never in prose. The reader cannot tell
  whether it is the compressed cold-reader sentence described in "Mechanism" or a separate field.
- **"the bank" / "the store"** — used interchangeably without either being introduced.
- **"promoted"** ("the entry stays raw evidence and is never promoted") — implies a lifecycle with
  states that is never described.
- **"the landing-rate work"** — referenced as a known programme; nothing in this file says what a
  landing rate is.
- **"move" vs "execution skill"** — introduced in the retraction as a settled distinction with a
  date, carrying real argumentative weight, but defined only in passing and by assertion.
- **"M-2121 M-2203 M-3384 M-2134"** — source identifiers with no resolvable referent for a stranger.
  Note they are also, uniquely in this file, space-separated rather than comma-separated; a reader
  cannot tell if that is meaningful.

---

## Summary
F08 is well-written and unusually honest — it names its own falsifier, admits it has no rate, no
control and no pre-registration, and argues *against* its own strongest reading. That honesty is
precisely what makes the FAIL clean rather than harsh: the prose already knows the claim outruns the
evidence, and the claim sentence was never brought back to match. **FAIL at step 2.** One anecdote
plus a self-scored number cannot carry a sentence in the generalising present tense. Scope the claim
to the witnessed instance, or file it as a hypothesis.
